**Kenneth Rathbun | Independent web developer**

For local service businesses getting their first website. Give customers a clear place to understand your services, see your work, and contact you or reach your existing booking service.

## Included in the build

- One main marketing page with agreed sections for your business, services, photos or approved reviews, hours or service area, and contact details.
- Design aligned with your existing business identity, with layouts for phones and desktop screens.
- Editing and organization of your supplied draft text.
- Clear phone, email, or existing booking-service links.
- Search titles and descriptions, plus mobile and accessibility checks.
- Two consolidated website revision rounds within the agreed scope: one after the initial design and one during pre-launch review.
- Deployment, connection to your domain, and a practical handoff identifying accounts and upkeep responsibilities.

## How delivery works

Agree the scope and schedule, supply materials, review the design and build, then approve the verified website for launch. You provide accurate business facts and approved images, and approve the final content. Timing is confirmed after reviewing scope, materials, and availability.
